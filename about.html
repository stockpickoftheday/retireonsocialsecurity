About this site

This site answers one question: how much Social Security will I get?

Social Security is the biggest source of retirement income for most Americans, and the rules around it — full retirement age, bend points, delayed retirement credits, break-even ages — are usually explained in government language. This site explains them the way you would explain them to a friend, and it does the math for you.

The calculator uses the same formula Social Security uses: your 35 highest-earning years, averaged into a monthly figure, run through the 90/32/15% bend-point formula, then adjusted for the age you claim. It shows your benefit at every age from 62 to 70, your break-even ages, and your lifetime totals at the life expectancy you enter.

The site is run by Cory Lewis. It is educational only — it is not financial advice, and it is not the Social Security Administration. Before you make a claiming decision, confirm the numbers on your own statement at ssa.gov/myaccount.

Everything on this site runs in your browser. There is no account to create and no sign-up. The numbers you type into the calculator are never sent anywhere.

Figures on this site were verified in November 2026. Benefit rules, bend points and tax thresholds change, so the numbers are checked and updated when they do.
