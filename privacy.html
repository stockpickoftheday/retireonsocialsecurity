Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing. The calculator runs entirely in your web browser. The earnings, birth year, and other numbers you type in are used on your own device to work out the answer, and are never sent to us or to anyone else. This site has no accounts, no sign-up, and no way to identify you.

Cookies and advertising

This site currently uses no cookies and shows no ads. In future, advertising may be shown through Google AdSense, which uses cookies to show ads that are relevant to you. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google's Ads Settings page. This policy will be updated if and when advertising is added.

Analytics

This site currently uses no analytics. If anonymous visitor statistics are added later, they will count visits only and will not identify individual visitors.

Links to other sites

This site links to ssa.gov, the Social Security Administration's website, so you can check your own statement. That link is the only link to another website on this site.

Contact

If you email us, we will use your email address only to reply to you. We do not sell, share or rent personal information to anyone, and we do not send marketing emails.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
